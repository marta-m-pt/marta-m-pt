Hi, I’m a graphic designer with an interest in digital design (UI), and the way digital design allows me to tell visual stories. The photographic process inspires much of what I do, as I am interested in capturing motion, light and detail in everyday life in cities.

I like to experiment with how digital tools and systems work, this is why I have such an interest in learning about technical/complex software, and creating aesthetically pleasing and well organized designs that are both functional and clear.
