Artigo Specimen

Category Typography
Year 2023
Client Academic Project

Artigo is an old style inspired typeface system for text. It was inspired by the handwriting aspect of the first roman types but it intends to be a contemporary interpretation. Its abilities are in small text with personality.

The italics capture a lot of its dynamic feeling, even more expressive on the display version that stands as the most handwritten one.
