Cartoon Network

Category Motion Graphics
Year 2025
Client Academic Project

This academic project involved creating a full branding package for a TV channel of our choice. I chose Cartoon Network and developed a series of animated assets, including bumpers, logo animations, and visual transitions.

The work captures the channel's playful and chaotic spirit through bold motion design, expressive character work, and vibrant colors — crafted to resonate with a young, energetic audience.
