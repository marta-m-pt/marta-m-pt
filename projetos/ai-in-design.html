AI in Design

Category Editorial & Web Design
Year 2025
Client Academic Project

"AI in Design" is an exploration of how artificial intelligence is transforming editorial and web design. This project includes in-depth research, interviews with designers, and a practical guide for integrating AI tools into the creative process.

The goal was to create an accessible resource that demonstrates both the opportunities and ethical challenges that AI brings to design. The result is a hybrid publication — part printed editorial, part digital platform — that serves as a starting point for designers who want to explore these tools.
